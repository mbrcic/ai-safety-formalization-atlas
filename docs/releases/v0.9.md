# v0.9 Release

- Prepared: 2026-11-14
- Published: 2026-11-14 to `main`; tag `v0.9.0`
- Publication status: **published**. The `v0.9.0` tag and its GitHub Release
  are the canonical publication record, and this note is not.

Package version: **0.9.0** (`lakefile.toml`, `CITATION.cff`).

Every count below was measured on the release tree on 2026-11-14, from the
ledgers and by the gate scripts named in the text. The working history behind
this release, with the dates each paragraph was written, is
`docs/releases/unreleased.md` as it stood at commit `0698d27b`.

## One-paragraph truth

This release widens the atlas and then spends most of its effort checking the
new material against print. Five clusters are new: Sovereignty, Goodhart,
Decision, LinearSystems and Composition. Wireheading grew from 8 to 20 module
files, and two survey rows, BY-001 and BY-002, reached `EXACT` through the
linear-systems work. The ledger grew with it. `registry.yaml` went from 91 to
144 results, and every one of the 53 new rows is an artifact row, so the 49
claim rows are the same 49. The statement-by-statement source audit went from
8 sources and 113 graded rows to 28 sources and 566 rows. Two debt counters
are now computed by scripts: scope debt stands at 4 owed cells, each costed in
writing, and witness debt at 31 theorems that no worked example applies, a
ceiling the gate enforces. The maintainer signed 34 AI-system bridge
declarations between 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-05; with the 3 reviewed before
v0.8.0, all 37 are signed, 30 of them at `REVIEWED`. Against that, the root import grew from
81 to 251 imports, 107 of them `Examples/` modules, and the plan to shrink it
before this release was not carried out.

## What changed since v0.8.0

**New mathematics.** Module-file counts include each cluster's facade file.

- **Sovereignty** (50 module files): Pauly's coalition logic,
  Goranko, Jamroga and Turrini's playability, Peleg's effectivity functions,
  the concurrent game frame, Wooldridge and van der Hoek's action systems, and
  six governance bridges. Each bridge is witnessed in
  `Examples/Sovereignty/Governance.lean`, with the escape shown wherever one
  exists.
- **Goodhart** (5 module files): Manheim and Garrabrant's regressional and
  extremal cases, Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's overoptimization result, and
  Skalse et al.'s hackability definitions.
- **Decision** (5 module files): a rewardless MDP carrier, a discounted value
  layer, and `Decision.BoundedValue`, which values a stationary policy on an
  arbitrary nonempty state type. The contraction argument behind it is
  Econlib's `Blackwell.lean`, ported under Apache-2.0 as
  `AISafetyAtlas.Analysis.Blackwell`. `vPiBdd_eq_vPi` proves the bounded and
  finite value functions agree wherever both are defined.
- **LinearSystems** (8 module files): Klamka's observability and
  controllability, with `determinesStateOn_iff_isObservable` and
  `isCompletelyReachable_iff_isControllable` proving each algebraic criterion
  equivalent to the dynamical property it names. This moved BY-001 and BY-002
  to `EXACT`.
- **Wireheading** went from 8 to 20 module files: Ring and Orseau's delusion
  box, the self-modifying agent, value learning, a stochastic corrupt-reward MDP
  with `PMF` transitions and policies, and goal preservation run over whole
  trajectories.
- **Fairness**: Kleinberg, Mullainathan and Raghavan's Theorem 1.2, the
  approximate trade-off, in `Fairness.ApproximateRiskAssignment` at print's own
  error function. The records are `RELATED` because several of §3's displays
  needed repair before a proof went through; the repairs are listed in
  `docs/provenance/kleinberg-fairness-tradeoffs.md`.
- **Containment**: Alfonseca et al.'s containment theorem (JAIR 2021) as
  `Verification.Containment.harming_undecidable`, a `REVIEWED` bridge graded in
  section 28 of the source audit. It gives BY-025 a `RELATED` in-tree record.
- **d-separation** is now in the tree. `Causal.DSep` consumes the graph layer
  of the external Causalean library, pinned by commit at `e298f649` after
  upstream replaced its history on 2026-10-06.
- **`atlas-check`** grew from 5 to 14 kinds. The nine new kinds are
  `access`, `conformity`, `enforcement`, `fairness`, `goodhart`, `membership`,
  `refusal`, `shield` and `unlearning`.

**Joins between notions of agent behaviour.** v0.8 named seven models with no
transports between them. Six of those seven now have an adapter module that
imports the `Decision.MDP` carrier: `Wireheading.CRMDP` directly, and
`Wireheading.AgentEquations`, `Wireheading.GoalPreservation`,
`Verification.Robot`, `Preference` and `Compositional.Hyperproperties` through
`AgentHistory`, `GoalPreservationCarrier`, `RobotRun`, `Trajectory` and
`TraceSystem`. `Wireheading.GoalPreservationSource` is the one without an
adapter. The knowability kernel has 16 consuming modules outside itself and
outside `Examples/`, up from 10 (`report_consumers.py --hub`).

**Ledger and grading.**

- **Source audit.** `docs/provenance/source-coverage-audit.md` grades 28
  sources and 566 statements: 300 `Yes`, 17 `Partial`, 208 `No` and 41 `Beyond`
  (`check_coverage_audit.py`). At `v0.8.0` it held 8 sources and 113 statements.
- **The fourteen unrowed modules are now accounted for.** The Sovereignty,
  Goodhart and Decision clusters were written on 2026-09-10 and merged in #72
  (2026-10-05); on 2026-09-10 they held 14 modules and no registry row. Today Goodhart has no unrowed module out of 5, Sovereignty has
  3 out of 50 and Decision has 2 out of 5. Across the whole root import, 144
  library modules are imported directly, 101 host an `IN_TREE` registry row,
  and the other 43 are each listed in the audit's module ledger with a reason:
  8 graded through a sibling module and 35 with no printed statement to grade.
  `check_coverage_audit.py` fails if a root-imported module has neither.
- **Scope debt is computed.** Four cells graded `Narrower` or `Mixed` are owed,
  and each declares that it is open and costed (`check_scope_owed.py`): Everitt
  et al.'s Definition 17 and Theorem 18, and Turner and Tadepalli's A.12 and
  A.13.
- **Witness debt is computed.** `check_witness_debt.py` reports 3 of 2,696
  pinned theorems ungrounded. Two of the three cannot be witnessed, because
  their antecedent type is proved empty by `isEmpty_o24Solution`, and
  `docs/status/witness-vacuity.json` names that proof. 31 theorems reach no
  `Examples/` application, on 20 leaves. Both numbers are pinned in
  `agent_gate.sh`.
- **Bridges.** All 37 `BRIDGE` declarations are signed: 30 `REVIEWED` and 7
  `STATEMENT_REVIEWED`. The maintainer signed 31 on 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04
  and 3 on 2026-10-05; the other 3 (BY-004, BY-012, BY-033) were reviewed before
  v0.8.0. Before signing, each allowed claim was narrowed to what its Lean
  statement proves.
  The label is per declaration, so the row-level count of reviewed source-claim
  interpretations stays at 3, with 1 statement-reviewed.
- **Triage.** `UNTRIAGED` rows went from 22 to 13 of the rows carrying no
  atlas Lean.
- **Source metadata.** A machine audit of source metadata and rights, with
  per-finding human dispositions, and missing source locators added (#69, #70).

**Process.**

- **Elaboration drift.** The 29 statements whose elaborated type changed when
  SCM domains became types have a recorded verdict in
  `docs/status/elaboration-accepted.json`, pinned by digest so a later change
  to any of them fails again (#75).
- **Kernel replay.** The nightly check replays each module once, in parallel,
  after every scheduled run from 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-12 timed out (#76). The
  drift dump is parallel too (#78).
- **Public API pin.** Since #72 the pin excludes `Examples/`, which are
  worked witnesses and carry no compatibility promise.

## Critical non-claims

- **No MAIS problem is newly claimed solved.** The conjecture board is
  unchanged at 3 open of 11. MAIS-O70's verification remains conditional on
  frontier hypotheses that are stated and not proved.
- **The two MAIS-O70 fidelity adjudications are still unsigned.**
  `docs/provenance/mais-o70-fidelity-adjudications.md` records both as
  drafts, and no gate script checks that file.
- **Headline claim coverage moved by two rows.** BY-001 and BY-002 reached
  `EXACT`, and BY-025 gained a `RELATED` in-tree record. Claim rows carrying
  atlas Lean went from 21 to 24, and `RELATED`-only rows from 8 to 9. Every new
  row is an artifact row. 24 of the 49 claim rows have no formalization record
  in any system.
- **A signed bridge licenses its stated conditional and nothing wider.** The 37
  signatures are scoped to the narrowed claims in each review file. None is a
  claim about a deployed system.
- **Sitting on one carrier is not a transport.** The six adapters project each
  model onto `Decision.MDP`. No theorem yet moves a result from one of the seven
  models to another.
- **The atlas grades nothing inside Causalean.** `Causal.DSep` states Everitt
  et al.'s Definition 6 over Causalean's graph layer, and the audit grades that
  statement in its section 8. Causalean's SCM layer is not used, because its
  domains are measurable spaces where the atlas's are finite.
- **The axiom audit does not grade a proof route**, for the reason v0.8 gave:
  `#print axioms` reads a declaration's type as well as its value.

## Known debts

- **The root import was meant to shrink in v0.9 and did not.** `STATE.md`
  recorded the plan on 2026-10-05: a small root of primitives and facades,
  per-cluster entry points, and `Examples/` off the root. The root still has
  251 imports, 107 of them `Examples/`, against 81 and 19 at `v0.8.0`.
- **The module-ledger check reads only direct root imports.** 138 library
  modules are not imported directly by `AISafetyAtlas.lean`, and 100 of them
  host no `IN_TREE` registry row, 54 of those in `SingularLearning`. No script
  asks whether those modules need a row.
- **Four scope cells are owed**, each costed. The maintainer ruled the
  null-context reading for Everitt's two on 2026-09-21, so what remains there is
  a measure-theoretic conditional expectation. The audit's own note argues
  against paying the cost of Turner and Tadepalli's two.
- **18 leaves of witness debt need a model**, beyond the 2 that cannot be
  witnessed; they are listed by `check_witness_debt.py`.
- **145 of 196 `Wider` and `Beyond` rows name no worked witness**
  (`check_scope_witnesses.py`, advisory).
- **The `GoalPreservationSource` model has no adapter** to the shared carrier.

## Objective evidence

Measured on this tree without a Lean build:

- `scripts/agent_gate.sh`: every validator, generated-view check, prose check
  and path check passes, including `check_statement_freeze.py` (411 graded
  statements unchanged) and `check_module_graded.py` (307 library modules, 0
  unreviewed).
- `pytest`: 532 of 542 tests pass. The other 10 shell out to `lake env lean`
  and need a built library, which this checkout did not have.
- No `sorry`, `admit`, `axiom` or `native_decide` token in the released Lean
  files (`validate_current_state.py`).

the off-root targets in `scripts/lean_build_targets.txt`,
`check_print_axioms.py`, `check_audit_coverage.py`, `axiom-audit`,
`check_atlas_check.sh` and `generate_declaration_index.py --check`.
