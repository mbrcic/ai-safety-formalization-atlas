# v0.10 Release

- Prepared: 2026-11-24
- Publication status: **prepared, not published.** The `v0.10.0` tag and its GitHub Release,
  once made, are the canonical publication record, and this note is not.

Package version: **0.10.0** (`lakefile.toml`, `CITATION.cff`).

Counts below were measured on the release tree on 2026-11-24 by the scripts named in the text.

## One-paragraph truth

This release regrades the statement-by-statement source audit and replaces the scale it was graded
on. Every graded row not already closed (315 of the 317 graded at v0.9.0) was regraded blind by several models and, where they split or
a ruling applied, adjudicated against the rendered source page and the Lean; the scope taxonomy is
now SPEC v6 ([`docs/guide/scope-grading.md`](../guide/scope-grading.md)), which grades three axes
(objects, applicability, conclusion) with a test each and computes scope and coverage from them. The
regrade found printed claims that are false as printed: 15, in 10 sources (12 statements, 3 steps of
printed proofs or constructions), are refuted in Lean (`docs/provenance/refutations/`), and the rows
are graded against repairs the author accepted; each refutation argues why its repair is the one
needed, and three leave open whether a weaker repair would also do. Two more files
refute readings the rulings exclude (Manheim and Garrabrant's regressional Goodhart without
independent noise; Melo et al.'s machine under partial correctness). The library grew to close what the regrade found: 17 new modules, and about
23,000 more lines of Lean under `AISafetyAtlas/`. Statement coverage moved from 300 `Yes` / 17 `Partial` / 208 `No` / 41 `Beyond`
to **311 / 4 / 209 / 42**; scope moved from 156 `Same` / 155 `Wider` to **108 `Same` / 201 `Wider`**,
and scope debt from 4 owed cells to **5**, each costed. The fall in `Same` is the regrade, not a
regression: v0.9 graded many generalizations as `Same` that the new tests read as `Wider`. Where a
generalization is `Same`, 42 rows carry a Lean proof that the atlas statement follows from print's
through print's conclusion (`docs/provenance/transfers/`, checked by CI).

## What changed since v0.9.0

**The audit and its scale.**

- `docs/guide/scope-grading.md` is the canonical taxonomy. `Same` and `Wider` are certified by
  entailment in each direction; a carrier generalization is representation (so `Same`) when it
  passes the transfer test (§7.1). Two grades are new: `None` (the cited Lean proves nothing of
  print) and `Refuted` (literal print refuted in Lean, no repair ruled). The old labels map as in
  `docs/provenance/regrade-2026-11/spec/MAPPING-to-v6.md`.
- Coverage is computed from scope (`Yes` iff `Same` or `Wider`); `check_coverage_audit.py` fails a
  cell that disagrees, and accepts the two new grades.
- 154 audit rows changed in the migration to v6, and the completion pass moved 31 grades (28 up to
  `Same`, 3 down to `Mixed`). Four rows that v0.9 owed (Everitt's Definition 17 and Theorem 18,
  Turner and Tadepalli's A.12 and A.13) are closed. Five rows are owed now, each with its cost in
  the audit: Ring and Orseau's Statement 4 (`None`: undecided at universal priors), an
  Armstrong–Mindermann row (`Mixed`, pending ruling R13), Ring and Orseau's Statements 1 and 3
  (`Mixed`: a joint specialization the cited theorems do not give), and Orseau and Ring's optimal
  agent `A^μ` (`Mixed`: the Lean lift fails on all histories).
- Provenance: `docs/provenance/regrade-2026-11/` (spec, rulings register and log, final grades, the
  v6 adjudications, Lean transfer and closure records). Raw grader transcripts, grading packets and
  intermediate grading rounds are not published.

**New mathematics.** Preference (9 modules: greedy-planner complexity under exact, rational and
timed codings, discounted and episodic variants, one-planner overrides), Decision (episodic value,
history-dependent occupancy), InformationTheory (countable data processing, mutual information as
KL divergence), Sovereignty (α- versus actual effectivity, truly playable representation), Causal
(discrete marginals), Wireheading (ties under a program prior). Among the closing lemmas: the mode
of `X` is the best guess made without information, randomized guesses included
(`errorProb_mode_le_of_indepFun`), and restriction from a composite system onto its apparatus is
surjective (`composite_restrict_surjective`).

## Critical non-claims

- **Hand-derived transfers.** 7 rows are `Same` on a transfer checked by hand through print's
  conclusion, not by a Lean lemma (author's ruling G11); they would be `Wider` under a Lean-only rule.
  Coverage is `Yes` either way.
- **Adjudicators.** The v6 pass adjudicated 115 rows, 78 by one model and 41 by another (073, 299
  and the cross-checked 254 and 277 by both), one model per row otherwise (`docs/provenance/regrade-2026-11/v6-impact/OUTCOME.md`). Rows
  whose grade moved below print were ruled on by the author or are listed as owed. The 20 rows that
  moved to `Same` on a newly written Lean transfer were not adjudicated again: the compiled transfer is
  the check, each was read for the E2 pattern, and two transfers were withdrawn after an independent
  review.
- **Row 299** (Klamka) is `Same` by the author's ruling that print leaves the field and the time
  domain open; its controllability and observability properties are still stated at real time and
  complex coefficients (the state and output equations are general).
- **Argued steps.** Four rows are `Wider, argued` (076, 148, 193, 277); their argued steps are
  described in the adjudication records, not as defect records in the audit (SPEC v6 §7.9 asks for
  the latter).
- **Open transfer candidates.** The blind grades proposed a transfer for 18 more rows graded `Wider`
  that is not established in Lean (8 not attempted, 4 failed, 3 rejected or withdrawn as truth-value
  maps, 3 waiting on a reading of "discrete state space"); they stay `Wider`
  (`docs/provenance/regrade-2026-11/v6-impact/OUTCOME.md`).
- **Axis columns and headline split.** SPEC v6 §11 asks the audit to record each row's axes; the
  table records the computed scope only, and the axes are in the provenance. The headline split of
  `Yes` into proved, argued, stated and provisional (§6) is not yet reported.
- Rulings still open: R13, R18 (`docs/provenance/regrade-2026-11/RULINGS-PENDING.md`).
