# v0.10.1 Release

- Prepared: 2026-11-25
- Publication status: **prepared, not published.** The `v0.10.1` tag and its GitHub Release,
  once made, are the canonical publication record, and this note is not.

Package version: **0.10.1** (`lakefile.toml`, `CITATION.cff`).

## One-paragraph truth

A patch release. The two printed claims of Wolpert 2018 that `v0.10.0` refuted
without a repair now have one proved beside the refutation. Proposition 12's
printed proof breaks at two steps, not one, and the repaired bound holds under
both conditions, each of which is needed. Example 6's computation stays refuted,
but the sharpness of Proposition 8's bound, which the example was meant to show,
is proved. No grade in the source audit changes; the audit counts are those of
`v0.10.0` (108 `Same` / 201 `Wider`, 5 owed cells). Witness debt stays at its pin
of 3.

## What changed since v0.10.0

- **Wolpert 2018, Proposition 12** (`Inference/Complexity/EntropyBound.lean`).
  Step 1 of the printed chain bounds a sum over probes by a sum over setups and
  needs the answering setups to be distinct; step 3 needs each to carry mass at
  least `1/|Γ(U)|`. `prop12_repaired` proves the printed conclusion under both
  (`HeavyAnsweringSelection`). Each condition alone has a countermodel
  (`prop12_distinct_insufficient`, `prop12_massBound_insufficient`), and a uniform
  model attains the repaired bound with equality.
- **Wolpert 2018, Example 6** (`Inference/Stochastic/Sharpness.lean`). Proposition
  8's bound is sharp at every `|Γ(U)| ≥ 1` (`prop8_bound_sharp`). For every device
  the construction has accuracy `(2 − n) · min_x E_P(Y | x) / n` (`example6_accuracy`)
  and attains the bound exactly when `n ≤ 2` or `E_P(Y | x)` is constant
  (`example6_attains_prop8_bound_iff`).
- The Wolpert 2018 map records both as `REPAIRED`, with the refutations kept
  (clashes 23 and 25); only Corollary 25 stays `REFUTED`. The registry row
  `LAND-WOLPERT-KNOW-DEFECTS-001` names the repairs.
- Worked examples apply each new theorem (`Examples/Inference/EntropyBound.lean`,
  `Examples/Inference/Sharpness.lean`).
- A worked use-site of `aggregateOffTrainingLoss_eq` (#81, CT-13), by an outside
  contributor.

## Critical non-claims

- The repair of Proposition 12 shows that each added condition is needed by the
  printed argument and that neither alone gives the conclusion. It does not show
  that either condition is necessary for the conclusion itself.
- The non-claims of `v0.10.0` ([`v0.10.md`](v0.10.md)) carry over unchanged.
