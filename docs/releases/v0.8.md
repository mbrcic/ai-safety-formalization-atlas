- Prepared: 2026-09-07
- Publication status: **prepared, not yet tagged.** The `v0.8.0` tag does not

Package version: **0.8.0** (`lakefile.toml`, `CITATION.cff`).

## One-paragraph truth

Two things happened since v0.7, and they pull in opposite directions. The atlas
took on a **community**: MAIS's open-problem agendas are now transcribed as Lean
statements, seven of them settled by the build, one candidate solution
conditionally verified against three named frontier hypotheses, and two printed
propositions refuted with machine-checked countermodels — which is a new mode for
this repository, grading problems it did not choose and solutions it did not
write. And the tree grew a **causal layer**, a fairness result, Ashby's counting
law with a runnable check, and a vendored doubly-efficient debate development,
all on a toolchain moved to Lean v4.33.0. That is breadth, and v0.7's own release
note named breadth as the risk: it recorded that one joint between domains is not
composition, and that seven notions of agent behaviour coexisted with no
transports between any of them. So the last third of this release is spent on the
other direction. The knowability kernel is now a hub with **10 consuming
modules**, four of them new — and `Causal` and `Compositional.Hyperproperties`,
which had no reference to it in either direction, reach it in one step; the trace
theory
that had defined `TraceSystem`, `IsKSafety` and a prefix topology for two releases
without anything in the tree producing a trace now has a producer; and anonymity —
previously implicit in the absence of identifiers from a structure — is a theorem
saying a node cannot recover its own identity from what it observes. The seven
notions still have **no transports between them**, and the non-claims below say so
in the same words v0.7 used.

## What changed since v0.7.0

**A community, and a new grading mode.**

- **The MAIS conjecture ledger.** Printed problems from the MAIS open-problem
  agendas are carried as compiling Lean statements with no proof, pinned to a
  source commit and per-file SHA-256. Seven are settled by the build. The rest
  stand as statements, which is the honest form: a problem the atlas cannot state
  carries no row at all rather than a row claiming less than it looks.
- **MAIS-O70, conditionally verified.** A submitted candidate solution was checked
  rather than assumed. The unconditional fragment is separated from three named
  frontier hypotheses that are *stated and not proved*, each enumerated with its
  consumers in [`frontier-manifest.md`](../provenance/frontier-manifest.md). The
  arithmetic gate is symbolic over all natural dimensions, not extrapolated from a
  finite search.
- **MAIS-O7 and MAIS-O77.** A refutation, an unconditional proof, and one
  assumption — with the O77(b) reduction showing the loss expansion terminates and
  comparability provably failing off the zero-block case.
- **Solutions others filed are graded apart from the artifact they are graded
  against**, in [`mais-solutions.md`](../status/mais-solutions.md), so "someone
  submitted this" and "the atlas checked this" cannot be read as one fact.

**New mathematics.**

- **A causal inference layer**: causal Bayesian networks, structural causal
  models, decision networks, margin classes, and behavioural identifiability.
- **The Kleinberg–Mullainathan–Raghavan fairness trade-off**, with both escape
  routes realized by concrete witnesses rather than stated as hypotheses —
  `equalRates_escape` and `perfect_escape` each pin a disjunct with a worked
  instance and a proof that the other disjunct fails there.
- **Ashby's counting law as an oversight bound**, backed by
  `Control.RegulationCheck.columnsInjective` and a fifth `atlas-check` kind. It is
  the first check added for a *hypothesis* rather than a conclusion: the law is
  settled, and what nothing tested was whether any table satisfies the column
  condition it quantifies over. One of the four worked tables fails the hypothesis
  and makes the bound false, so a `false` verdict can never be read as a clearance.
- **Doubly-efficient debate**, reproduced and vendored behind a facade, kept off
  the root import.

**Interoperability — the last third, and the part v0.7 asked for.**

- **The knowability kernel became a hub.** `report_consumers.py --hub` lists
  **10** modules consuming `AISafetyAtlas.Knowledge` from outside itself and
  outside `Examples/`. Four are new this release: `Preference.Knowability`,
  `Compositional.Knowability`, `Compositional.Hyperproperties.Knowability` and
  `Causal.Knowability`.

  The baseline is **not** one. At `v0.7.0` seven files outside `Knowledge` and
  outside `Examples/` already referenced it, among them `Oversight.JointObservation`
  and its `Coverage` and `Residual` modules, `Wireheading.ObservationLimits`,
  `Inference.Device`, `Inference.PhysicalKnowledge` and `SelfAwareness`. What was
  one at v0.7 is the count v0.7's own note gave — *transports between two
  domains*, of which `Knowledge.Devices` was the only one. Consuming modules and
  transports are different counts and this release does not conflate them: what
  is new here is that `Compositional.Hyperproperties` and `Causal`, which had no
  reference to the kernel in either direction, now have one.
- **Four names, one proposition, and the tree now says so.** `Knowable`,
  `Determines`, `Covers` and `Refines` all unfold to `∃ f, ∀ x, B x = f (A x)`;
  `Covers q h` and `Refines q' q` are *definitionally* the kernel at the
  candidate's projections. The names mark use, not content, and the docstrings say
  which use. Two lemmas that had re-proved kernel results by hand now apply them.
- **The trace theory has a producer.** `Hyperproperties` had defined
  `TraceSystem`, `Realizes`, `IsBadObservation`, `IsKSafety`, the
  self-composition reduction and a prefix topology, and nothing in the tree
  produced a trace; four modules generated executions that nothing consumed as
  traces. `Compositional.NetworkTraces` connects the first of them, in both
  readings — the outside view (a run as the sequence of whole configurations) and
  the node's-eye view (what one node observes, round by round).
- **Anonymity is now a statement.** `not_knowable_node_of_fixedPointFree`: in an
  anonymous network with a symmetric start, a node cannot recover *which node it
  is* from its own observation trace. That is what "anonymous" means in Angluin's
  model, previously left implicit in the absence of identifiers from the
  `Algorithm` type. `no_unique_leader_from_obsTrace` then strengthens the leader
  impossibility: the predicate may read a node's entire history and still cannot
  single one out.
- **Escape routes gained content.** `FORMALIZED` routes rose from 1 to 3; a route
  the ledger can only name is marked `NAMED_ONLY` rather than counted as one the
  atlas proves.

**Toolchain and process.**

- **Lean v4.33.0**, Mathlib `db584cd6`, PFR `7d6404b7`, Foundation `30a16ffa`.
  Proofs changed and statements did not: 262 graded signatures and 1703 public
  names unchanged, with every one of the 31 statement-level differences classified.
- **Every row with no atlas Lean now carries a `statability` verdict** saying why,
  and the validator fails without one — so the map of what is missing cannot
  quietly develop holes.
- **The local gate now checks the declaration index** that eight of its own
  scripts resolve names against. That check existed and ran only in CI, so the
  local gate had been reporting green while resolving against a stale environment.

## Critical non-claims

- **No MAIS problem is claimed solved.** The conjecture rows that remain open are
  open. MAIS-O70's verification is **conditional** on three frontier hypotheses
  that are stated and not proved, and the atlas holds no unconditional inhabitant
  of the P1/P2 targets.
- **Two MAIS-O70 fidelity adjudications are unsigned.** The project's rule is that
  agents draft and a human adjudicates against print. Both verdicts in
  [`mais-o70-fidelity-adjudications.md`](../provenance/mais-o70-fidelity-adjudications.md)
  currently stand as agent drafts, and **no gate script checks that file**. Any
  grade depending on them inherits that provisional status. This is recorded here
  rather than resolved.
- **The seven notions of agent behaviour still have no transports between them.**
  `Wireheading.CRMDP`, `Wireheading.AgentEquations`, `GoalPreservation`,
  `GoalPreservationSource`, `Verification.Robot`, `Preference` and
  `Compositional.Hyperproperties` remain seven models. Four clusters now *project
  into* the shared kernel, which is a hub and not a unification; the count of
  transports between the seven is unchanged at zero. v0.7 said this and it is
  still true.
- **The axiom audit does not grade a proof route.** `#print axioms` traverses a
  declaration's *type as well as its value*, so a statement mentioning a
  classically-built object reports that object's axioms whatever its proof does —
  measured: a freshly written, entirely pure definition in
  `Oversight.JointObservation` reports `Classical.choice`, through `Finset`. The
  audit's count says no declaration rests on a *custom* axiom across a named set,
  and `check_audit_coverage.py` is what bounds that set. Neither number grades an
  argument. Recorded in
  [`verification-scheduling.md`](../provenance/verification-scheduling.md).
- **22 of the 35 rows with no atlas Lean are `UNTRIAGED`** — nobody has read the
  source against the tree. That is a labelled absence, not a claim about
  difficulty.
- **`atlas-check` decides models, not systems.** A verdict is about the JSON model
  it was given; the agreement theorems say the runner matches the Lean.
- **The causal layer has no d-separation.** Identifiability is stated over
  behavioural equality of masked gap responses; the general graphical machinery is
  not in the tree, and depending on an external development for it remains a cost
  to price rather than a decision taken.
- **The Oversight `VarietyBound` bridge is prepared and unsigned.** The statement
  review is argued, the maintainer decision is not made, and the row stays
  `HUMAN_REVIEW`; the reviewed-bridge count does not include it.
- **New shared laws were landed ahead of consumers, deliberately.**
  `Networks.step_equivariant`, the two kernel spokes and the trace producer have
  no consumer outside their own modules yet. They are visible in the
  `report_consumers.py` retire-or-consume queue and are prunable. The
  consumed-outside-`Examples/` share therefore *fell*; under this policy that
  queue is a pruning worklist, not a quality score.
- **One model choice in `NetworkTraces` is the atlas's, not Angluin's.** A node's
  own state sequence is not everything it could see — `Algorithm.update` reads
  incoming messages and the state sequence does not determine them. `ObsTrace` is
  the coarsest honest choice, not the finest possible one.
- **No new reviewed bridges from the interoperability work.** Nothing here
  licenses a claim about a deployed system.
