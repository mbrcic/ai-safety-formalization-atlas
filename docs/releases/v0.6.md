## One-paragraph truth

A feature release built around one question: what can an observer recover about
a system it is part of. `AISafetyAtlas.Knowledge` is the answer's kernel —
knowability as a decoder, with the no-collision characterization proved rather
than assumed — and six specializations build on it: embedded measurement,
composition and finite bridges, time indexing, fibre ambiguity, self-reference,
and ambiguity accumulation over a window. Two existing domains became consumers
rather than restating the argument. Breuer's 1995 self-measurement propositions
are formalized at their abstract set-theoretic core and graded `EQUIVALENT` —
every statement §3.5 displays, including the Corollary carrying his Gödel
analogy — with apparatus construction, dynamics, quantum treatment, and EPR left
out and said so. `AISafetyAtlas.SelfAwareness` mechanizes the survey's own §4.3 —
Proposition 4.7 and Theorem 4.8 — at `EQUIVALENT` after first-author
statement-level review. The public page ships. `v0.5.1` claimed no change to
public API, statement-match grades, or theorem statements; this release changes
all three, which is why it is a minor bump and not a patch.

## What changed since v0.5.1

- **A knowability kernel with consumers.** `LAND-KNOW-001` gives `Knowable` in
  decoder form, `Determines` ordering observations by informativeness, and a
  negative certificate. Joint observation's coverage laws and the CRMDP
  complement pair are now theorems over that kernel rather than parallel
  arguments.
- **Breuer, at the layer that is actually general.** `LAND-SELFMEAS-002` is the
  abstract measurement core — reading sets, inference maps, meshing,
  Propositions 1–2 — with Proposition 1 proved twice, once axiom-free.
  `LAND-SELFMEAS-003` *derives* proper inclusion from a product complement or a
  finite cardinality gap instead of assuming it, and exhibits the bijective
  positive boundary.
- **BY-044 formalized.** `AISafetyAtlas.SelfAwareness` exposes the source
  sketch's non-cancellation step as a strict positive awareness-cost law,
  separates the constituent obstruction from the maximal-composite proof, and
  permits ordinary awareness cycles — inhabited and witnessed.
- **Four layers over the kernel.** Time-indexed knowability, fibre ambiguity,
  the model as a component of the state it models, and ambiguity accumulation
  across a window.
- **Ledger machinery.** Typed adjacency (`relations`, `result_shape`) with
  validator guards, and `NC-002`…`NC-007` recording every absence claim
  machine-readably — including `NC-005`, which is a correction to a prose claim
  that the search disproved.
- **Navigation contracts.** Facade parents are grouped by what one import
  supplies; module-specific examples mirror their module's path.
- **A public page.** `site/` plus a Pages workflow that deploys from `main` only.

## Critical non-claims

- **No new reviewed bridges.** The count stays at 2 (BY-012, BY-033). BY-044 is
  `STATEMENT_REVIEWED`: its encoded statement is accepted and its AI-system
  interpretation is withheld, because none has been proposed. Nothing here
  licenses a claim about a deployed system.
- **Breuer is `EQUIVALENT`, not `EXACT`.** Every statement §3.5 displays is
  mechanized, but the hypotheses are deliberately not the source's: meshing
  alone is assumed where the paper assumes meshing and surjectivity. Physical
  apparatus construction, dynamics, the quantum treatment, EPR, and §3.4's
  continuity route are omitted. Physical *bridges* are a separate artifact row
  and are not retroactively Breuer's theorem.
- **Physical containment is not the obstruction.** Nothing claims that every
  contained subsystem has a nontrivial remainder or a cardinality gap; those are
  explicit hypotheses.
- **Self-knowledge limits are contemporaneous.** `LAND-CL-001` reproduces
  Chandy–Lamport as the boundary: a process *can* determine a consistent global
  state while computation continues.
- **BY-044 is not a consciousness result**, not a diagonal argument, and not a
  prohibition on partial self-monitoring or on cyclic architectures.
- **Definition 4.6's model-simplicity clause is unformalized**, and recorded as
  such in the provenance note.
