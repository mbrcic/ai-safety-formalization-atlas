## One-paragraph truth

A depth release on one source and a structural release on everything else.
Wolpert's *Physical limits of inference* is now the largest body of mathematics
in the tree — inference devices, weak and strong inference, control, complexity,
stochastic devices, and the 2018 physical-knowledge cluster — carried at the
source's own quantifiers rather than at convenient specializations, with every
finiteness restriction the encoding did not need removed and every place the
printed text fails to determine a formal statement recorded as a numbered clash.
Supporting that required a probability substrate the atlas did not have: finite
mass functions, entropy, mutual information and KL divergence, with Mathlib
`PMF`/`Measure` interoperation. Structurally, the release does three things the
atlas had never done: it **joins two domains** — `Knowledge.Devices` transports
between the knowability kernel and Wolpert's devices, in both directions, without
identifying them — it makes the kernel **runnable** by a consumer who cannot read
Lean, via `Knowledge.Check` and the `atlas-check` executable, each backed by a
Lean theorem that the runner agrees with the proof, and it puts **every domain**
under a generated dependency view instead of the two that happened to be under
work. Finally, every source backing Lean in the tree was re-checked against its
published text, which found and fixed a real citation defect.

## What changed since v0.6.0

- **Wolpert 2008 at print scope.** `AISafetyAtlas.Inference` is now 25 modules:
  devices, reduced form, weak and strong inference, mimicry, control, inference
  complexity, stochastic devices, and physical knowledge. Statements are widened
  where the proof never used the restriction — Theorem 4 with no finiteness on
  either setup range, Definition 6 over a countable target range, Theorem 7(i)
  without its universe restriction, `C-hat` over an arbitrary setup range,
  Definition 10 over countably supported setups of finite entropy.
- **The source read as a source, and corrected where it is wrong.** Every row
  was checked against the LaTeX sources and against the Elsevier article, not
  only the preprint. Where the printed text does not determine a formal
  statement, the ambiguity is a numbered clash recording which reading the Lean
  encodes. Where the printed statement is **false**, it is refuted with a
  machine-checked countermodel: Corollary 1(ii) of the 2008 paper is false as
  printed and needs a proper subset the statement omits; in the 2018 preprint,
  Proposition 12 fails on a four-state countermodel meeting every printed
  hypothesis, Example 6's sharpness claim does not hold, and Corollary 25's
  target is inadmissible under its own equation (11). Of the 46 tracked 2008
  statements, **42 are `SOURCE-EXACT`**, one is specialized, two are repaired and
  one is refuted, with none unmechanized.
- **A probability substrate.** `FinPMF`, entropy sums, mutual information and
  KL divergence, with `PMF` and `Measure` interoperation — built because the
  2008 and 2018 material needs it. It is *shaped* to be independent of either;
  nothing outside `Inference` uses it yet, and the non-claims below say so.
- **The first joint between domains.** `Knowledge.Devices` transports between
  `Knowledge.Knowable` and Wolpert inference devices in both directions, under
  explicit hypotheses, and **deliberately does not identify them** — the
  non-identification is itself witnessed both ways.
- **The kernel became runnable.** `Knowledge.Check` supplies executable
  checkers with agreement theorems, and `atlas-check` (`Main.lean`) is a
  command-line tool that decides knowability, coalition coverage and device
  questions from a JSON model. Twelve checks are asserted to agree with the Lean
  proofs, plus a scaling guard.
- **One scenario that is a job.** `Examples.Oversight.Overseer` runs the
  transport on an overseer that may reconfigure — one overseer answers every
  probe yet its report stream is not knowable; the other's report factors
  through its setup and is refuted at every configuration.
- **Navigation for the whole tree.** Dependency views are generated for all
  thirteen domains, and CI regenerates and diffs them.
- **The executable is in the trust scan.** `Main.lean` is covered by the same
  `sorry` / axiom checks as the library.
- **Published numbering, not report numbering.** Everitt, Filan, Daswani and
  Hutter is cited as the published AGI 2016 **Theorem 12** (the chapter proves
  nothing and delegates to technical-report Theorem 16). Van Leeuwen and
  Wiedermann's published chapter reuses the report's numbers for different
  statements; a collision table records that. Wolpert 2018 has no peer-reviewed
  version; the registry says so.

## Critical non-claims

- **No new reviewed bridges.** The count stays at 2 (BY-012, BY-033). Nothing
  here licenses a claim about a deployed system.
- **The knowability kernel and Wolpert's devices are still not the same thing.**
  `Knowledge.Devices` transports between them under stated hypotheses; the
  non-identification of `Knowable` with `WeaklyInfers` and with
  `PhysicallyKnows` is witnessed in both directions and is not withdrawn.
- **`atlas-check` decides models, not systems.** A verdict is about the JSON
  model it was given. The agreement theorems say the runner matches the Lean, not
  that any model matches a real deployment.
- **Wolpert 2018 rests on a preprint.** No published version exists; the atlas
  reads `arXiv:1711.03499v3`, and this is the only source in the tree where a
  preprint is the authority.
- **BY-033 matches the unrefereed technical report**, not the published chapter,
  whose Theorem 1 is strictly stronger and carries a regularity hypothesis the
  report's does not.
- **`RELATED` on BY-024 is about row coverage, not transcription depth.** The
  survey row cites four works; the atlas transcribes Wolpert 2008 in full and the
  2018 preprint substantially, and leaves Wolpert 2001 and Devereaux et al.
  untranscribed. The grade follows from that, and from the presence of repairs,
  specializations and refutations — not from shallowness. The per-statement
  record is the accurate instrument.
- **The relationship vocabulary cannot express "corrects the source."**
  `EXACT`/`EQUIVALENT`/`RELATED` assume the source is right and grade fidelity to
  it. A formalization that is stronger than print in some places, weaker in
  others, and refutes the source in a third set has no single correct label. This
  is recorded as a limitation of the vocabulary, not resolved.
- **The recorded clashes are not proof gaps.** Each is a place where the printed
  source does not uniquely determine a formal statement; each records which
  reading the Lean encodes.
- **Resource-bounded complexity is still absent**, which is why Armstrong's
  Proposition 10 is not formalized and why MAIS-O1 and MAIS-O16 remain not
  statable against `AISafetyAtlas.Logic.loeb`.
- **The depth is not yet vocabulary.** `Inference` is 55% of the library
  (12,332 of 22,625 lines) and, outside its own subtree and its own examples, has
  exactly **one** library consumer: `Knowledge.Devices`. The probability
  substrate it introduced — `FinPMF`, entropy sums, mutual information, KL
  divergence — has **no** consumer outside `Inference` at all. That is depth on
  one source, not a shared primitive, and it is recorded here rather than left to
  be discovered. Making the substrate usable without importing Wolpert is
  identified work, not done work.
- **One joint is not composition.** `Knowledge.Devices` is the first transport
  between two domains and remains the only one. Seven distinct notions of agent
  behaviour coexist in the tree — in `Wireheading.CRMDP`,
  `Wireheading.AgentEquations`, `GoalPreservation`, `GoalPreservationSource`,
  `Verification.Robot`, `Preference` and `Compositional.Hyperproperties` — with
  no transports between any of them.
