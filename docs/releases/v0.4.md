## One-paragraph truth

v0.4 turns the atlas's workbench framing into substantial importable formal
capital across three connected domains: compositional reasoning,
wireheading/self-modification, and preference deduction. It adds operational
hyperproperty topology and self-composition, rectangularity and local-contract
boundaries, anonymous-network symmetry, finite Ring–Orseau equations,
corrupt-reward complement constructions, a finite-percept goal-preservation
induction step, and Armstrong–Mindermann preference-complexity and override
cores. These are kernel-checked results with concrete applicability witnesses
and statement-by-statement source maps. They are deliberately not presented as
complete paper reproductions. Headline `EXACT`/`EQUIVALENT` coverage remains
**7 of 44**, and no AI-system bridge graduates in this release.

## Critical non-claims

- **Not paper-complete.** A1–A3, B1–B3, and B7 are scoped cores,
  specializations, generalizations, or source dependencies. Their exact deltas
  are in
  [`a1-a3-b1-b3-b7-statement-maps.md`](../provenance/a1-a3-b1-b3-b7-statement-maps.md).
- **Not full Everitt Theorem 16.** B2 assumes the continuation domination that
  the source derives using modification independence and Theorem 20; it proves
  a finite-percept one-step result, not the all-times theorem.
- **Not exact Everitt Theorem 11.** B3 fixes one deterministic transition,
  supplies extrema as structure fields, and uses the continuous unit interval
  instead of the source's finite reward grid.
- **Not BY-043.** A3 supplies an Angluin-style symmetry dependency, not the
  survey's embodiment theorem; inverse-port covering machinery and randomized
  symmetry breaking remain absent.
- **Not the full preference-deduction paper.** Conjecture 9 is a predicate,
  never an axiom; Proposition 10 is absent; no nontrivial reasonable-language
  witness is claimed.
- **Not a system safety case.** No new result is promoted to a reviewed
  AI-system bridge, and no theorem establishes a property of a deployed
  system.

The consolidated residual record is
[`a1-a3-b1-b3-b7-reverification.md`](../provenance/a1-a3-b1-b3-b7-reverification.md).
